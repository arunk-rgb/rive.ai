FAQ

How does Rive understand my operations?

Rive builds a continuously evolving knowledge graph of your operational environment by connecting to the systems you already use: SAP, Maximo, Oracle, IoT gateways, and your documents. Each signal is normalized against a shared ontology and linked to the assets, changes, and people around it, so a sensor reading connects to the work order it triggered, the part it affects, and the vendor who supplies it. The result is persistent operational context that updates in real time and gets sharper the longer Rive runs.

Does Rive replace my ERP or CMMS?

No. Rive sits on top of SAP, Maximo, Oracle, and the systems you already run, and uses their data to build and maintain its knowledge graph. Those systems were built as systems of record for humans to query. Rive is built for a world where operations coordinate and act on their own. It amplifies and unifies your existing investments rather than replacing them.

Why isn't a dashboard enough?

Dashboards are where insights go to die. Predictive alerts get produced but never acted on; optimization models suggest changes no workflow can implement. Operating a plant requires continuous understanding of a live system: what changed, what depends on what, what failed before, and what is happening right now. That understanding can't come from a static report. Rive's knowledge graph gives agents live operational context so they can reason and act, not just display.

What can Rive do autonomously?

Rive can detect anomalies, trace failures across sites, identify likely root causes, prioritize work orders by real production risk, propose fixes, and carry out operational workflows on your behalf. It starts with read access so it can build and maintain the knowledge graph. From there, you control what actions it is allowed to take. By default, changes still route through your existing approval flow, whether that's a work-order sign-off, a maintenance plan, or a confidence threshold you set.
